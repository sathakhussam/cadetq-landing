Delays & manpower planning

Every late cadet is a gap in your manpower plan.
Catch the slip where it starts.

CadetQ flags cadets running over their benchmark at each stage, records the cause (weather, aircraft, instructor availability or the cadet), and keeps the follow-up on record until they're back on plan.

Stop discovering delays. Start managing them.

Most programmes find out a cadet is behind at the end of a phase, when the time is already lost. CadetQ compares hours and days with your benchmark as flights are logged, so the slip shows up the week it happens.

  • Planned vs logged hours at every milestone, with variance bands
  • Cadets over benchmark, flagged automatically

Every delay has a cause. Now it has an owner.

Weather, aircraft, instructor or cadet, on record and followed up until they're back on plan.

  • Delay categories on every over-benchmark flight
  • Notes, review status and full delay history

Handovers & documents

No cadet stuck between teams.

Each move from one team to the next comes with paperwork. CadetQ tracks FTO onboarding, CPL and FRTOL checklists, conversion flying, licences and medical validities, so the next team knows exactly what's outstanding.

  • FTO onboardingSubmissions, review status and cadet summaries.
  • CPL & FRTOL document checklistsShortfalls and resubmissions, document by document.
  • Conversion flyingRecency calculator, workflow and checklist.
  • Licences & medicalsIssue and validity dates, flagged before they lapse.

Trust & governance

The right access for every team. Records that hold up in an audit.

Every change is logged with who made it, when, and the value before. Access is set by role and by batch, so each team and each FTO sees only what it should.

  • Full audit trailEvery change to a record: who, when, and the data before and after.
  • Role-based permissionsEach role sees and changes only the modules it needs.
  • FTO teams & batch accessWho at each FTO can access which batch, by person or by team.
  • Private & shared roomsFTO and internal teams each have a private room, plus a shared one.
  • Secure document storageEvery cadet's documents in their own controlled folder.
